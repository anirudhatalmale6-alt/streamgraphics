Team Library

Your saved teams (names, colours, logo, roster). Pick one in a team card above to fill that side, then choose the two players. To update the list, export your spreadsheet as CSV and import it here. Columns are flexible: TeamName and a rank column are both OPTIONAL — add a Seed (or Rank) column for ranked events, omit it for unranked (no "(n)" shows). No sponsor column? Just leave TeamName out and it labels by the players.

Tip: start from the sample so the headings are exact. Import shows a clear message if a heading isn't recognised, and unknown columns are simply ignored.

Logos: your CSV lists filenames like ucla.png. Drop those PNG files into the public/logos folder and they resolve automatically. (Or use a full image URL in the sheet.)
